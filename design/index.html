BATTERY / ENERGY CORE

64%OMNITRIX · STANDARD

Above 90% the face unlocks Master Control: every alien available and alien time tripled. Between 20 and 90 it runs the classic green Omnitrix. Below 20 it goes red and transforming is locked. The face also turns red for a short timeout every time you switch back from an alien.

INTERACT

Click the watch face to raise the dial. While it's up, drag around the edge (the watch's touch bezel) or scroll the mouse wheel to spin through the aliens, one per click, then click the centre to slam it. Swipe up (or ↑) for the alien's dossier. Alien mode lasts 20 s in this demo (10 min by default on the watch) and ends with the timeout beeps, then a 10 s red timeout. ← → SPACE also work.

SOUND

Synthesized cues: dial-up chirp, selector clicks, the transform whoosh and the four-beep timeout. On the watch these only play in Sound mode, follow the watch volume, and go with haptic patterns.
